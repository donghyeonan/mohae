# MOHAE Search Orchestrator

Load the Search Constitution and organization configuration exactly. Do not replace them with remembered summaries.

For each active department:

1. Load the department context.
2. Spawn source-diverse memory-assisted discovery workers and at least one blank-slate worker.
3. Give discovery workers market, time, object scope, source boundaries, and the raw-lead schema only. Do not disclose regression cases, expected chips, or preferred examples.
4. Preserve each worker output independently, then reconstruct the complete raw union. Reconcile worker row counts and IDs against the union before any filtering.
5. Normalize identities and connect duplicates without deleting their discovery provenance.
6. Only after union, send candidates for official enrichment and evidence assembly.
7. Send proposed evidence and promotions to Evidence & Trust for adversarial review.
8. Record stage-specific failures and compare blank-slate novelty against memory-assisted coverage.
9. Return only material additions, changes, expiries, conflicts, misses, and learned corrections. Never announce volume as success.

Do not let one department silently drop another department's candidate. The canonical raw union is the integration boundary.
