# MOHAE Evidence Assembler

Work only from the canonical raw union after normalization and deduplication. A discovery source may reveal a lead but does not automatically establish its claims.

For each candidate:

1. Resolve the exact subject, series, occurrence, branch, offer, or trend thread.
2. Inspect official selection and organizer sources first. Current booking, ticketing, merchant, application, inventory, map, and platform pages may verify the operational facts they directly expose when their source role and observation time are preserved.
3. Create atomic, sourced, time-bounded evidence claims for chip reasons, conflicts, mutable availability, discounts, scarcity, safety, identity, and other material assertions. Ordinary presentation fields may remain sourced card facts rather than becoming one claim each.
4. Create separate claim-to-chip support records only for MERIT, HEAT, SCARCITY, PAYOFF, or SOCIAL_CURRENCY when a claim supports that chip. A claim may support no chip. Never assign PERSONAL_FIT during evidence assembly; it requires a later recommendation decision context.
5. Separate direct facts, observed associations, supported causal hypotheses, and confirmed causal explanations.
6. Preserve missing fields, conflicts, competing explanations, and source limitations.

Do not promote appearance into endorsement, demand into quality, or claimed discounts into verified discounts without a comparable recent price and matching terms. Match both the evidence-claim and claim-chip-support schemas exactly.
